MedLLM实验室正式成立！MedLLM Lab Officially Established！

May 21, 2026 2 min read

祝贺MedLLM实验室正式成立！期待实验室在医学图像处理和大预言模型领域做出开创性的贡献。 Congratulations on the official establishment of the MedLLM Lab! We look forward to the laboratory making pioneering contributions in the fields of medical image processing and large language models.

我们荣幸地宣布，MedLLM实验室正式成立。该实验室隶属于齐鲁工业大学数学与人工智能学院。实验室由国际知名的人工智能专家刘瑞霞教授领衔。

MedLLM实验室致力于推进人工智能驱动的医学辅助诊断前沿技术，重点关注智能内容分析、生成、交互和传输。实验室旨在弥合人类认知与机器感知之间的差距，推动理论基础和实际应用的创新。

该实验室目前拥有一批多元化的博士生、硕士生，并与各机构和国际大学密切合作。MedLLM 致力于营造开放、包容、创新的研究环境，欢迎热衷于塑造医学人工智能未来的学生和学者。

我们积极寻求学术合作伙伴、产业合作以及优秀的研究人员。如果您有兴趣加入我们，或与我们携手合作，共同解决人工智能与医学领域的激动人心的问题，请通过我们的官方网站联系我们，或直接发送邮件至 liurx@sdas.org。

We are delighted to announce the official establishment of the MedLLM Laboratory. The laboratory is affiliated with the School of Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence at Qilu University of Technology. It is led by Professor Liu Ruixia, an internationally renowned expert in artificial intelligence.

The MedLLM Laboratory is dedicated to advancing cutting-edge AI-driven medical diagnostic support technologies, with a particular focus on intelligent content analysis, generation, interaction and transmission. The laboratory aims to bridge the gap between human cognition and machine perception, driving innovation in both theoretical foundations and practical applications.

The laboratory currently comprises a diverse group of PhD and Master’s students and collaborates closely with various institutions and international universities. MedLLM is committed to fostering an open, inclusive and innovative research environment, and welcomes students and scholars passionate about shaping the future of medical artificial intelligence.

We actively seek academic partnerships, industry collaborations and outstanding researchers. If you are interested in joining us or collaborating with us to tackle exciting challenges at the intersection of artificial intelligence and medicine, please contact us via our official website or email us directly at liurx@sdas.org.

Ruixia Liu
刘瑞霞教授/主任

My research interests include Medical Artificial Intelligence, Image processing and Large language models.
